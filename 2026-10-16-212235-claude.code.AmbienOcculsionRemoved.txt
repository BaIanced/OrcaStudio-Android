● Haptics: settings tabs only get a tick when you swipe sideways between pages
  (SettingsEditor.kt:313, the TextHandleMove buzz). Tapping a tab gives nothing.
  The Models back/forward edge swipes have a tick too, which you've confirmed.
  The plate picks have a long-press haptic (OverlayParts.kt:138). So on the
  phone, test by swiping the settings page body, not by tapping the tabs.

  AO vs shadows: they're two different effects, and the shadow map didn't
  replace AO.
  - AO is a screen-space pass that darkens creases and contact areas
    (SceneEffects.kt:239, setting ambientOcclusion, default off).
  - Shadows are the separate light-cast shadow, with its own toggle
    (contactShadows, Viewport.kt:205) and the light/shadow sliders.

  AO is still wired and works, but it's mostly redundant now, and it's off by
  default. I haven't looked at what it adds on top of the shadows by eye, so
  that's untested. I can remove the AO row, the ambientOcclusion setting and the
  MCP ao parameter, or keep them. I'd remove it unless you like the look. Tell
  me which.
