## Full description

OrcaStudio runs the OrcaSlicer engine on your phone, tablet or Chromebook. It uses the same printer, filament and process profiles as the desktop program. CHECK: "same results" has been shown only on the developer's own comparisons.

What you can do
- Slice without a PC. The engine runs on the device. No server, no cloud slicing.
- Fix a downloaded model: make a hole bigger, thicken a wall, lengthen a tab, widen one side of a part, cut, hollow, repair.
- Make simple parts: drag shapes together, cut holes, round edges, add text. Enter numbers when you want to.
- Print: send to Bambu Lab printers and to Klipper (Moonraker), OctoPrint, PrusaLink and other hosts. Watch the print and the camera from the Device tab.
- Use a big screen: keyboard, mouse and pen work on Chromebooks and tablets.

Good to know
- Needs Android 10 or newer on an arm64 device. CHECK: minSdk and ABI list in Gradle.
- Pre-release, shared as is, no warranty. Check your G-code before long prints.
- Signed Bambu Lab printing has been used on a real printer. Moonraker and MKS hosts were tested against mock hosts only. Other hosts are untested on real hardware.
